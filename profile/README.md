<h2>
  <a href="https://www.thebluealliance.com/team/11070">Who are we?</a>
</h2>

<img
  src="https://github.com/RoboRexFRC/.github/blob/main/profile/RoboRexLogo.png?raw=true"
  width="120"
  height="120"
  align="left"
  alt="RoboRex Logo"
>

We are Team RoboRex #11070 from the Drom Hasharon Regional Council.
Our team was founded on July 1st, 2025, following the closure of the
school's robotics program in the middle of the year. The initiative
was created to allow students to continue participating in robotics.
The team consists of about 40 students from grades 9 to 12, who work
together through cooperation, hard work, and a shared desire to learn
and develop. A central part of the team's activity is working with the
community by exposing students to the world of STEM through activities
and workshops that encourage curiosity and interest in science and
technology.

<h2>
  <a href="https://www.firstinspires.org/">What is FIRST Robotics?</a>
</h2>

<img
  src="https://github.com/RoboRexFRC/.github/blob/main/profile/FIRST_Vertical_RGB_reverse.png?raw=true"
  width="172"
  height="129"
  align="right"
  alt="FIRST Robotics Logo"
>

FIRST Robotics is a nonprofit organization founded by inventor Dean
Kamen in 1989 to inspire young people's interest and participation in
science and technology. FIRST stands for "For Inspiration and
Recognition of Science and Technology." The organization hosts several
robotics competitions each year, giving students from around the world
opportunities to demonstrate their engineering and problem-solving
skills.
